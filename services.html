What we offer

Five services, one experienced team.

Every engagement is scoped clearly, delivered by senior practitioners, and backed by evidence — not just a checklist.

Website Security Audit

A thorough, one-time review of your website's security posture — misconfigurations, outdated components, exposed data, and weak access controls — with a clear, prioritized remediation report.

Website Security Maintenance (Monthly)

Ongoing monthly monitoring, patching guidance, malware scanning, and security hygiene checks so your website stays protected between audits — not just at the start.

Web Application VAPT

Manual and tool-assisted vulnerability assessment and penetration testing of your web applications and APIs — covering the OWASP Top 10 and business-logic flaws automated scanners miss.

Incident Response

Rapid containment, investigation, and recovery support when something goes wrong — breach triage, evidence preservation, root-cause analysis, and a clear path back to normal operations.

Security Retainer

Priority, on-call access to the UcyAegis team for ongoing advisory, periodic testing, and rapid response — so you have expert security support available whenever you need it, not just once a year.
